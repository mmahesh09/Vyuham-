## Product
Vyuham is a web app for AI-assisted dashboards. You upload a CSV or Excel file, ask a question in plain English, and get the answer plus a chart.

**Live today:**
- CSV/XLSX upload and a DuckDB query engine
- Chart builder with 11 chart types
- "Story feed" dashboards: plain-English insights, each with a chart and a comparison to the previous period
- KPI row, filters and share links (public read-only dashboards)
- SQL Lab
- Canvas layout
- Alerts and scheduled digests (shown in the app; email delivery is logged and not yet sent)
- Team roles and tasks
- Templates: Sales, Startup metrics, Expenses
- Report exports: PDF of a dashboard, CSV and Excel

**Coming next (roadmap, so NOT positioning attributes):** Claude-powered Ask and auto-dashboards, Google Sheets connector, email delivery, billing.

**Pricing idea:** flat price per company, not per user. Free plan: 1 dashboard, 5 MB. Pro and Team prices not set yet.

**Stage:** free beta / pre-launch (founder hasn't confirmed which), solo founder.
