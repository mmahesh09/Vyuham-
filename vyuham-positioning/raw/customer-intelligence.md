## Jobs to Be Done
### Functional jobs
- Produce a client-ready monthly report for each client with minimal hands-on time
- Explain changes ("why did leads drop?") in plain language
- Connect ad spend to the client's real outcomes (CRM leads, sales, revenue)

### Social jobs
- Look competent and proactive in front of the client; "prove value"
- Look bigger and more professional than they are (white-label, branded reports)

### Emotional jobs
- Avoid the dread of "reporting week"
- Feel safe that the client won't churn because they can't see results

## Best-Fit Customer Profile
[Opinion, from the patterns above] The agencies that feel this most:
- Run lead-generation or e-commerce performance campaigns, where the client cares about sales and leads, not impressions
- Have clients whose real outcomes sit in CRM exports, Shopify/WooCommerce exports, Excel sales sheets, or Indian lead platforms (IndiaMART, JustDial) that ad-reporting connectors don't cover
- Have 5–20 clients: past the point where hand-made reports are bearable, and price-sensitive to per-client tools

## Switching Triggers
### What triggers a search
- Adding clients until reporting week eats billable time
- A client asking "but what did this do for sales?"
- A Looker Studio connector breaking, or a connector bill rising

### What triggers the actual switch
- A trial that produces a client-ready report fast
- Pricing that doesn't grow with each client

### What prevents switching
- Existing templates and client habits; annual contracts
- **Missing native integrations**: this is the evaluation criterion buyers mention most

## Key Insights for Positioning
1. Buyers evaluate agency reporting tools mainly on **integrations, white-label and automation**, which are exactly Vyuham's gaps today. Head-to-head, Vyuham loses the first comparison. [Opinion, from evaluation words and G2 cons]
2. The pain incumbents serve least is **proving business outcomes with the client's own data**, which arrives as spreadsheets. That matches what Vyuham does natively (any CSV/XLSX, several datasets, SQL joins). [Opinion; needs validation]
3. AI commentary is now expected and isn't a differentiator. Vyuham's insights are currently rule-based, which is fine for accuracy but can't be marketed as "AI" against these competitors. [Data on competitors; Opinion]
