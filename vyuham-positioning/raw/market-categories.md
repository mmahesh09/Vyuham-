## Candidate Categories

### 1. Agency client reporting software (head-to-head)
- **What buyers expect:** 30–80+ native ad and analytics connectors, white-label portals, scheduled automated reports, and (since 2026) AI summaries and "ask your data" [Data: vendor homepages, roundups]
- **Leaders:** AgencyAnalytics (7,000 agencies), Swydo (2,500+), DashThis (18k+ users), Whatagraph (1,000+) [Data: vendor claims]
- **Dynamic:** mature and crowded; price competition on the pricing model (per client vs per source vs per dashboard); AI is a race everyone is already in [Opinion]
- **Fit for Vyuham:** **Poor today.** Vyuham fails the first three checks buyers make (connectors, white-label, AI). It would be judged as a weaker AgencyAnalytics. [Opinion]
- **Type:** head-to-head. Not winnable now.

### 2. "Chat with your spreadsheet" AI data analysts (Julius AI, ChatGPT data analysis, Rows, Quadratic, Powerdrill)
- **What buyers expect:** upload a CSV and get instant AI answers and charts; $15–20/mo per user [Data: 2026 roundups, vendor-written]
- **Dynamic:** crowded and cheap, and ChatGPT ($20/mo) sets the bar [Data]
- **Fit:** Vyuham's upload-and-ask flow matches the category, but its Ask is rule-based today, so it would lose a head-to-head on how smart the answers are. Saved dashboards, scheduled digests, share links and teams are things these tools lack, but buyers in this category don't look for them first. [Opinion]
- **Type:** head-to-head. Weak.

### 3. Small-business BI / dashboards (Zoho Analytics, Databox, Power BI, Looker Studio)
- **What buyers expect:** connectors to business apps, dashboards, low cost. Zoho Analytics from about ₹24,000/yr (2 users), strong in India through Zoho One [Data: Business Today, Apr 2025; partner pages, conflicting]
- **Fit:** reasonable, but broad and dominated by Zoho in India and free Looker Studio globally. Segment 3 (agencies) isn't the natural buyer here. [Opinion]
- **Type:** head-to-head. Weak for a solo founder.

### 4. Subcategory: client results reporting from the client's own spreadsheets (big fish, small pond)
- **Definition:** dashboards and monthly reports built from **whatever files the agency and client already have** (ad-platform CSV exports *plus* the client's sales, lead or CRM sheets, e.g. Shopify/WooCommerce exports, Tally/Excel sales, IndiaMART/JustDial lead lists), joined together and explained in plain English, at one flat price.
- **Why it's open:** incumbents treat CSV as a "manual fallback" [Data: vendor pages]. Outcome tools (Cometly, Databox + HubSpot, Triple Whale/Polar) reach revenue only through **native integrations** with US-centric stacks (HubSpot, Stripe, Shopify) [Data]. Clients whose outcomes live in Excel or Indian tools aren't served by connectors at all. [Opinion]
- **Buyer expectations it sets:** "works with any spreadsheet", "shows the client's real numbers", "explains changes", "shareable report". **Vyuham does all of these today**: multi-file upload, DuckDB/SQL joins (SQL Lab), story-feed insights with period comparison, share links, PDF/CSV/Excel export. [Data: codebase]
- **Risks:** smaller pond; monthly CSV uploads are manual work; demand not yet validated. [Opinion]
- **Type:** subcategory (big fish, small pond).

### 5. Category creation ("client outcome intelligence", "AI analyst for agencies")
Not recommended. It would cost a solo founder too much to educate buyers, and AgencyAnalytics already uses "client intelligence". [Opinion]

## Recommendation
Position as a **subcategory of agency client reporting**: client reporting **built on the client's own spreadsheets**. Buyers already understand "client reporting", and the qualifier says why Vyuham differs in a way it can deliver today. [Opinion]
