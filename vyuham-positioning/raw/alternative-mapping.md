## The Job to Be Done
Every month, an agency account manager has to show each retainer client "what we did, what happened, and why it matters", in a form the client will actually read. That means pulling numbers from ad and analytics platforms, laying them out, and writing commentary. The deeper job for the agency owner is **keeping the retainer**: proving that the spend turned into business results (leads, sales, revenue), not just clicks. [Opinion, based on vendor and agency sources below]

A second, under-served part of the job is connecting ad metrics to the **client's own business data**: CRM leads, closed sales, Shopify orders, offline sales, and Indian lead sources like IndiaMART and JustDial. That data mostly lives in spreadsheets and CRM exports, not in the tools' native connectors. [Data: WhatConverts, SaaS Hero, Codingclave; Opinion on the gap]

## Direct Competitors

### AgencyAnalytics
- **Type:** Direct competitor (market leader for small and mid-size agencies)
- **Hired for:** automated, white-label client reports and dashboards across 80+ marketing integrations
- **Strengths:**
  - About 7,000 agencies; 4.7/5 on G2 (457 reviews) and 4.8 on Capterra [Data: AgencyAnalytics homepage; G2]
  - AI Summary widget (whole-report summaries with custom prompts, Jan 2026) plus Ask AI ("Ask anything about your clients' performance") [Data: AgencyAnalytics changelog and feature page, vendor-stated]
  - White-label client portals
- **Limitations:**
  - Per-client pricing: $20/client/mo annual, $25 monthly; volume pricing via sales from 25+ clients [Data: Coupler.io, Swydo blog, Madgicx, 2026; third-party]
  - Top G2 cons: "Integration Issues" (25 mentions), "Limited Features" (14); reviewers mention inflexible reporting [Data: G2, Capterra]
  - Locked to its own dashboards (no Looker Studio or Sheets output) [Data: Porter Metrics, a competitor]
- **Best for:** PPC/SEO agencies with 5–50 clients who want everything in one branded portal
- **Switching triggers:** cost rising with the number of clients; a missing integration; needing custom calculations
- **Switching costs:** Technical: M (rebuilding templates) | Contractual: M (annual plans) | Emotional: M (clients used to the portal)

### DashThis
- **Type:** Direct competitor
- **Hired for:** simple, quick white-label marketing dashboards
- **Strengths:**
  - 30+ integrations plus a **CSV File Manager** for custom data [Data: DashThis homepage; Swydo comparison]
  - AI Insights included ("one-click analysis... wins, issues and opportunities"); AI Insights Pro adds chat [Data: DashThis homepage]
  - Unlimited users on every plan; from about $44/mo [Data: DashThis homepage, Nov 2026]
- **Limitations:**
  - Priced by number of dashboards and data sources since March 2026 [Data: DashThis blog; pricing tracker]
  - Few preset templates, limited custom calculated metrics, weak at blending data from several sources into one metric [Data: Whatagraph review (a competitor), TheCMO]
  - Pricing gets expensive with many clients [Data: G2 complaint summary]
- **Best for:** small agencies that want simplicity over flexibility
- **Switching triggers:** dashboard-count pricing as clients grow; the need for blended metrics
- **Switching costs:** Technical: L–M | Contractual: L–M | Emotional: L

### Whatagraph
- **Type:** Direct competitor (moving upmarket)
- **Hired for:** cross-channel reports with a governed data layer and AI agents
- **Strengths:** calls itself an "Agentic marketing intelligence platform"; agents build reports from a prompt or example; AI summaries in 18 languages; 1,000+ agencies and brands [Data: Whatagraph homepage; Porter Metrics]
- **Limitations:** high entry price (about €249/mo by one source; another lists the Max plan at $812/mo); client-facing AI chat reportedly only on Max [Data: Porter Metrics; Toolradar, Nov 2026; Swydo (a competitor). Prices conflict]
- **Best for:** mid-size agencies with many channels and the budget for them
- **Switching triggers:** price; complexity for small teams
- **Switching costs:** Technical: M | Contractual: M–H | Emotional: M

### Swydo
- **Type:** Direct competitor
- **Hired for:** automated reports and monitoring, priced per data source
- **Strengths:**
  - **"No per-client fees"**: €62/mo base (annual) with 10 data sources, then €4.50 down to €2.00 per extra source [Data: Swydo homepage]
  - AI summaries, "Ask Your Data", an agentic report builder, and an MCP server so clients' data can be used in ChatGPT and Claude [Data: Swydo homepage]
  - 2,500+ companies in 80+ countries [Data: Swydo homepage]
- **Limitations:** custom data goes through a CSV upload or Google Sheets, both manual [Data: Swydo comparison pages]
- **Best for:** SEO/PPC agencies that are price-sensitive about the number of clients
- **Switching costs:** Technical: M | Contractual: L | Emotional: L

## Adjacent Alternatives

### Looker Studio (+ a paid connector: Supermetrics, Windsor.ai, Porter)
- **Type:** Adjacent tool. It's the free default, and connectors cost extra.
- **Hired for:** flexible, free dashboards on Google data; other sources go through paid connectors (Supermetrics Starter about $49/mo for 3 accounts per source)
- **Strengths:** free, unlimited seats, flexible; widely recommended for agencies with fewer than about 10–15 clients [Data: several 2026 guides]
- **Limitations:** you build and style everything yourself; connections break; white-labelling is limited; non-Google sources need paid connectors [Data: DashThis case studies, Hackceleration, Balistro]
- **Switching triggers:** broken connectors and the hours of maintenance as clients grow
- **Switching costs:** Technical: M (existing dashboards) | Contractual: L | Emotional: M (it's free)

### General BI tools (Power BI, Tableau, Metabase)
Rarely used by agencies of 3–30 people because they need setup and skills. Not a real alternative for this ICP. [Opinion]

### Chat assistants (ChatGPT/Claude with a pasted CSV)
An emerging alternative for writing commentary: export the data, paste it, get a summary. [Assumption; no adoption data found. Swydo's MCP server suggests vendors expect this]

## Manual / Status Quo Alternatives

### Google Sheets/Excel + Slides/PowerPoint, written by hand
- **Hired for:** full control over a client-ready deck
- **Cost:** about 2–5 hours per client per month (HubSpot: 4–5; Enterprise DNA: 2–4; Growth Rocket: 4–6 before automation, under 90 minutes after); outliers of 8–10 [Data: vendor-influenced estimates, Tier 2–3]
- **Switching trigger:** the number of clients grows past what account managers can handle; reporting week eats billable time

### Doing nothing / platform screenshots
Sending ad-platform screenshots or native reports. Common with very small agencies and low-retainer clients. [Assumption]

## Key Insights
1. **Category leaders already offer every capability in Vyuham's planned story** for agencies: AI summaries, "ask your data", automated scheduled reports and white-label. They also have native ad-platform connectors, which Vyuham lacks. [Data]
2. **Per-client pricing is a real but partly answered pain:** Swydo ("No per-client fees") and DashThis (unlimited users) already market around it. A flat price is a weak sole differentiator. [Data]
3. **Every incumbent treats CSV/custom data as a second-class fallback** ("adds manual work"). None leads with combining the client's own business data with ad data. [Data: vendor pages; Opinion on the gap]
4. **Blending data from several sources into one metric is a known weak spot** (DashThis), and proving ROI needs CRM or sales data matched to campaigns. [Data: Whatagraph review, WhatConverts, SaaS Hero]
