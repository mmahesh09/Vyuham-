## Rungs already taken on the mental ladder
- **"Agency client reporting"**: AgencyAnalytics (leader, 7,000 agencies, highest rating)
- **"Simple, small-agency reporting"**: DashThis
- **"AI / agentic reporting"**: Whatagraph (agentic), Swydo (agentic builder, MCP), AgencyAnalytics (Ask AI)
- **"No per-client fees / everything included"**: Swydo
- **"Save hours / no busywork"**: claimed by all four

## Where messaging and buyer perception differ
- Every vendor leads with **time saved and automation**. Buyers' review complaints focus on **integrations and flexibility** (G2 cons). The time-saving message is saturated. [Opinion]
- No vendor's homepage leads with **the client's business results** (sales, revenue, CRM leads) or **working with whatever data the client has**. CSV is mentioned only as a fallback (DashThis). [Data: homepages; Opinion on the gap]

## Implications for Vyuham
- Don't claim "AI reporting", "save hours" or "no per-client fees" as the headline. All are taken, and some aren't true yet (Vyuham's Ask and insights are rule-based today). [Opinion]
- The open rung is around **"the client's own numbers"**: reports built from whatever spreadsheets the client and agency already have, joined together and explained in plain English. [Opinion; validated in Wave 2]

## Red Flags
- Vyuham lacks the three table-stakes features buyers check first: native ad-platform connectors, white-label, and live AI.

## Yellow Flags
- The open rung may be open because demand is small. Validate it.
